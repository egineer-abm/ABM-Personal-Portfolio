BakeryOS is being shaped around real family-bakery workflows. It combines durable SQLite records for inventory, sales, customer accounts, supplier bills, and invoices with an agentic sense-plan-act-observe loop. Natural-language instructions can handle retail sales, wholesale dispatches, stock checks, dynamic pricing, and merchant reconciliation without replacing the owner’s judgment.
