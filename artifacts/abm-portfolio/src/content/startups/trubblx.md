Training content usually asks you to watch. Trubblx asks you to participate.

### The idea

- Short interactive sessions instead of long passive videos
- Immediate feedback while the attempt is still fresh
- Progress you can actually feel, not just a completion percentage

Trubblx carries the same thread as everything else here: tools that make knowledge easier to reach without making the person using them less curious.
