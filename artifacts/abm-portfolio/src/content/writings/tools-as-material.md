date: A working draft

excerpt: The interesting question is less which tool won, and more what became possible after it arrived.
---

Every new tool quietly redefines what counts as a reasonable thing to attempt. The printing press did not just speed up copying; it made literacy worth having. Spreadsheets did not just replace ledgers; they made financial modelling a habit for millions.

AI tools are arriving in that same spirit — less as replacements and more as material. The interesting design question is what becomes buildable when a rough idea can become a working prototype in an afternoon.

This draft gathers examples where the material changed the builder, not just the build.
