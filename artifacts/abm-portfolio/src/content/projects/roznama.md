Roznama is designed around the feeling of writing in a personal notebook, while keeping the affordances of a developer tool. Slash commands, fuzzy matching, related-note mapping, Git sync, and integrations for Claude Code, Gemini CLI, and Cursor keep the archive useful without requiring cloud AI.
