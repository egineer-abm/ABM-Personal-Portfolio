An agent that teaches starts with a different first move than an agent that answers. Before explaining anything, it needs a picture of who is asking: what they already know, what they are trying to build, and where they tend to get stuck.

This note collects observations from building the Agentic Learning-Mentor skill — how a learner model changes the ordering of explanations, why production-grade code is sometimes the wrong first example, and what happens when the agent asks one good question before giving ten good answers.
